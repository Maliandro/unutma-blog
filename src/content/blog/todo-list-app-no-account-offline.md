Most to-do apps ask for an email before you can write "buy milk". Then the list lives on their server, and the app works offline only until it needs to sync. If you are searching for a **to-do list app with no account that works offline**, you probably want the opposite: open the app, write the task, and know it stays on your phone.

This page does two things. First, it gives you a **5-minute test** you can run on any app, ours included, to check whether "no account" and "offline" are real or just marketing. Second, it lists exactly what works offline in Unutma and what doesn't, because a couple of things depend on your phone rather than on us.

## Why most to-do apps need an account

An account usually means your tasks are stored on a server so they can sync between devices. That is a real benefit. It also means the app needs that server. Two popular examples, checked October 2026 on their own help pages:

| App | Account needed? | Offline, in their words |
|---|---|---|
| **Todoist** | Yes, a Todoist account | "Make sure you're logged in to your Todoist account before going offline." Offline changes sync when you are back online ([Todoist Help](https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/use-todoist-while-offline-4rbaZw)) |
| **Microsoft To Do** | Yes, a Microsoft account. Free with a personal one ([Microsoft Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/which-accounts-can-i-use-microsoft-to-do-with-b0eef0e0-9077-435c-9d65-0fff24e2766e)) | Work or school accounts need an Exchange Online mailbox so To Do can sync your data (same page) |
| **Google Keep** | Notes are saved to your Google Account ([Keep Help](https://support.google.com/keep/answer/10431250?hl=en)) | "Offline access to notes is available on Keep mobile apps, but not on your computer" ([Keep Help](https://support.google.com/keep/answer/10183819?hl=en)) |
| **Unutma** | No. There is no sign-up screen | Works fully offline. There is no server, so there is nothing to sync |

These are good apps. If you need your list on a laptop or shared with someone, an account-based app is the right choice. We compare one of them in detail in our [Google Keep alternative](/blog/google-keep-alternative-no-account-offline/) page, including where Keep wins.

## The 5-minute test for "no account" and "offline" claims

Run these checks on any app before you trust it with your life admin:

1. **Fresh install, first screen.** Install the app and open it. Does it ask for an email, Apple/Google sign-in or a phone number before you can add a task? If "Skip" is hidden or missing, it is not a no-account app.
2. **Airplane mode, then add.** Turn on airplane mode and add three tasks, edit one and complete one. Does anything show "waiting to sync" or a spinner?
3. **Airplane mode, then restart.** Close the app completely and open it again, still offline. Are all three tasks still there?
4. **Search the settings for "account", "sync" or "cloud".** If there is a sync toggle, your data goes somewhere unless you turn it off.
5. **Read the App Store privacy label or Play data-safety section.** Look for "Data linked to you". It is self-reported by the developer, but it tells you what they admit to collecting.

An app that passes 1 to 3 really is offline-first. Step 4 tells you whether it stays that way.

## What works offline in Unutma, and what doesn't

| Feature | Offline? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| To-do, shopping and wishlist lists | ✅ Yes | Shopping list is unlimited on the free plan |
| Reminders and routines | ✅ Yes | Notifications are scheduled on the phone itself |
| Notes, journal, budget, Vault | ✅ Yes | Journal and Vault are encrypted on the device |
| Typed assistant ("spent 40 on gas") | ✅ Yes | Runs on the phone |
| Voice input | ⚠️ Depends on your phone | Unutma uses your phone's own dictation (Apple or Google). It stays on the phone when your phone supports offline recognition for that language |
| Moving to a new phone | ⚠️ Manual | There is no cloud copy. You create a password-protected backup file (Premium) and open it on the new phone |
| Using it on a computer | ❌ No | Unutma is an iPhone, iPad and Android app only |
| Sharing a live list with someone | ❌ No | You can send a one-off copy of a list as a file, but it doesn't update live |

The ⚠️ and ❌ rows are the price of having no account. Make sure you are fine with them before you switch.

## The one habit a no-account app needs

With no server, nobody can restore your tasks if your phone is lost. That includes us. So the one habit worth having is an occasional backup file saved somewhere you control. Our step-by-step guide to [moving Unutma to a new phone](/blog/move-unutma-to-a-new-phone/) shows where people usually go wrong (hint: don't email the file to yourself).

## Free plan limits, stated plainly

Unutma is free to use with limits, and Premium removes them:

- **Unlimited:** the assistant, quick actions, shopping list and budget.
- **Capped on the free plan:** 25 open to-dos (completed ones don't count), 25 notes, 3 routines or reminders, 3 Vault entries, 10 wishlist items, 10 journal entries a month.
- **Premium** (monthly, yearly, or a one-time lifetime purchase) removes the caps and adds the backup file, CSV/PDF export, statistics and mini games. Every feature is open for the first 7 days.
- Nothing you wrote is ever deleted or hidden if you don't upgrade.
