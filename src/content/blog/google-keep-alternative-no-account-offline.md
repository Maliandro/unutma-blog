Google Keep is good at what it does: fast notes, checklists, and everything synced to your Google Account. People usually start looking for a **Google Keep alternative** for one of three reasons. They do not want their notes tied to a Google Account. They want something that keeps working with no signal. Or Keep has turned into a pile of sticky notes, and what they actually need is somewhere for to-dos, reminders and the "did I lock the door?" question.

This page is for the first two groups, and partly for the third. Unutma is our app, so we are not neutral. To make up for that, every claim about Keep below links to Google's own help pages or App Store listing, and **the table includes the rows where Keep beats us.** If one of those rows matters most to you, stay with Keep.

## Who should stay with Google Keep

Stay with Keep if any of these are true:

- **You write notes on a laptop.** Unutma has no web or desktop version. Keep runs in any browser.
- **You share a grocery list with your partner** and want to see items checked off live. Unutma cannot do that.
- **You want a reminder when you arrive somewhere.** Keep offers location reminders and Unutma does not.
- **You want to pay nothing, ever, with no limits on note count.** Keep is free. Unutma's free plan has limits (details below).

None of those is a small thing, and we would rather you choose the right tool than switch and feel let down.

## What is free and what is Premium (exact numbers)

Unutma's free plan has limits, and you should know them before switching:

- **Unlimited for free:** the assistant, quick actions, the shopping list and the budget.
- **Free up to a cap:** 25 open to-dos, 25 notes, 3 routines or reminders, 3 Vault entries, 10 wishlist items, and 10 journal entries per month.
- **Premium:** removes those caps and adds the backup file for moving to a new phone, plus CSV and PDF export, statistics and the mini games. Plain data export (JSON) is free.
- **Your data is never deleted or hidden** if you stay on the free plan or your trial ends.

Every feature is unlocked for the first 7 days, so you can test it properly before deciding.

## Moving from Keep: a 15-minute plan

Unutma does not import Keep notes automatically, and we don't want you to think it does. Here is the low-effort way to move:

1. **Don't move everything.** Open Keep and pick only the notes you opened in the last month. Most people find that's fewer than 20.
2. **Checklists become Lists.** Retype grocery and to-do checklists into the Shopping or To-Do list. Or say them: "buy eggs, bread and coffee" turns into three items.
3. **Anything with a password or PIN goes into the Vault**, not into a note.
4. **Recurring notes ("water plants every Sunday") become routines**, so they remind you instead of waiting to be found.
5. **Keep Keep for a month.** Leave Keep installed until you're sure. Nothing forces you to delete it.
